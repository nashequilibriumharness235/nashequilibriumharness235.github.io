📦 What Is This?

ai-visibility-skill is a free, no‑setup tool that checks if the biggest AI chatbots — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity — mention your brand when asked about your industry or topic. It then shows you exactly what those AIs say, so you can improve your brand's share of voice in generative search results. Think of it as an SEO audit, but for the AI era.

You don't need an account, a database, or any programming experience. Just download, run, and follow the on‑screen prompts. Everything runs on your own computer using only built‑in Python libraries.
